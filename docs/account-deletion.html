Request account deletion

What to include

Send the request from the email address associated with your account and include the name used in the app. Do not include passwords, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What happens next

We will use the request to identify the account, delete the account and associated personal data where required, and contact you if clarification is needed. Some records may be retained when required by law or to resolve disputes, as described in the Privacy Policy.
